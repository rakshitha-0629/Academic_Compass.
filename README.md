# Academic Compass
Transforming Academic Information into Action
Academic Compass is an AI-powered Student Operating System that centralizes academic communications, automatically categorizing and routing information into dedicated hubs for tasks, study materials, competitions, and AI assistance.

## Problem Statement
Students receive important information through emails, Google Classroom notifications, competition announcements, assignments, and academic circulars.
This information is often scattered across multiple platforms, causing students to miss:
1. Assignment submissions
2. Competition deadlines
3. Study materials
4. Important academic opportunities

## Problem Statement
Students receive critical information across fragmented channels like emails, Google Classroom notifications, circulars, and competition announcements. Important deadlines and opportunities often get lost, leading to missed assignment submissions, competition deadlines, and study resources.

### 💡 Solution & Key Features
#### 🏠 Home Dashboard: 
Centralized view of daily priorities, upcoming deadlines, active competitions, and study progress.

#### ✨ AI Inbox: 
Intake interface that automatically parses academic communications, extracts deadlines, classifies categories, and routes actionable tasks.

#### 🏆 Competition Hub: 
Tracks hackathons, workshops, and competitions with progress checklists and an interactive Competition AI Mentor.

#### 📚 Study Hub: 
Subject-wise repository featuring automated notes management, flashcard generation, quiz creation, and study assistance.

#### 🤖 Student Copilot: 
Unified agentic assistant with cross-hub context aware of all deadlines, assignments, and priorities.

## Workflow
Academic Communication -> AI Inbox -> Classification -> Routing -> Competition Hub / Study Hub / Dashboard ↓ Student Copilot Guidance
